<!-- feature-intro -->
Zip Pay adds Zip’s payment flow to Craft Commerce. Offer the gateway at checkout, hand the customer to Zip for approval, and bring the result back into Commerce’s order and transaction history.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Zip at checkout

Configure the gateway in Craft Commerce and make it available to eligible carts during checkout. The customer completes the hosted Zip flow while Craft Commerce remains responsible for the order.
<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-section -->
## Sandbox testing

Use sandbox credentials to verify redirects, successful payments, cancellations, and failures, then switch the configured gateway to production when the store is ready.
<!-- feature-section-end -->
