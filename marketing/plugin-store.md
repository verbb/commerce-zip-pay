Zip Pay adds Zip’s payment flow to Craft Commerce. Offer the gateway at checkout, hand the customer to Zip for approval, and bring the result back into Commerce’s order and transaction history.

Configure the gateway in Craft Commerce and make it available to eligible carts during checkout. The customer completes the hosted Zip flow while Craft Commerce remains responsible for the order.

## Features

- **Commerce gateway:** Offer Zip Pay through the checkout payment methods.
- **Hosted approval:** Send the customer through Zip’s payment authorisation flow.
- **Return handling:** Bring approved, cancelled, or failed attempts back to the store.
- **Transaction records:** Keep the outcome attached to Craft Commerce’s order history.
- **Sandbox testing:** Exercise the integration without creating live charges.
- **Gateway configuration:** Manage account credentials and environment through Commerce.
- **Sandbox testing:** Use sandbox credentials to verify redirects, successful payments, cancellations, and failures, then switch the configured gateway to production when the store is ready.
